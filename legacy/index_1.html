Walk Locomotion Use W A S D or Arrow keys on desktop. Use the on-screen joystick on mobile.
Looking Around Hold left-click and drag the mouse, or click the canvas to enter pointer lock. On touch devices, drag the right side of the screen.
Product Inspection Click or tap a product to see its price, details and a 3D preview you can turn with your finger or mouse. Want to turn the object in the room itself? Turn on Rotate selected object in Settings, then drag or use Q / E.
